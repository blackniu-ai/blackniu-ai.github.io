Crush Atlas · 心動圖鑑

使用說明與支援

從關係出發，用知識樹與關鍵字搜尋，快速查看常見可能、觀察重點與尊重界線的下一步。

快速開始

  1. 先選擇你們目前是「不認識」或「認識」。
  2. 再依關係類型與熟悉程度進入相符情境。
  3. 也可以在首頁搜尋行為關鍵字，例如「一直看我」或「已讀不回」。
  4. 閱讀時請一起查看常見可能、接下來觀察、適當下一步與界線提醒。

常見問題

搜尋不到內容怎麼辦？

縮短關鍵字，改用行為本身的詞語，或返回首頁沿著關係知識樹瀏覽。第一版內容會持續擴充。

需要登入或網路嗎？

不需要。主要知識內容隨 App 提供，可以離線瀏覽與搜尋。

如何更改語言？

本 App 支援繁體中文與英文。請前往 iPhone「設定」中的 App 語言選項切換；實際選項依 iOS 版本而定。

內容可以證明對方喜歡我嗎？

不可以。任何單一行為都不能證明真實心意。請結合關係、情境、時間與多個一致訊號，並尊重拒絕、隱私與同意。

問題回報

若遇到畫面、文字或功能問題，請寄信給我們，並盡量附上：

  • App 版本與 iOS 版本。
  • iPhone 型號。
  • 發生問題前的操作步驟與看到的錯誤訊息。

為保護你與他人的隱私，請勿寄送私人對話紀錄、未經同意的他人照片、住址、電話、帳號密碼或其他敏感個人資料。

Help & Support

Start with the relationship, then use the knowledge tree or keyword search to explore common possibilities, observation points, and respectful next steps.

Quick start

  1. Choose whether you are currently acquainted.
  2. Select the relationship type and familiarity level that best fit.
  3. Or search for a behavior keyword from the home screen.
  4. Read the common possibilities, what to observe, a respectful next step, and the boundary reminder together.

Frequently asked questions

Why are there no search results?

Try a shorter behavior-focused keyword or browse from the relationship tree. The first version's knowledge coverage will continue to grow.

Do I need an account or internet connection?

No. The primary knowledge content is included with the app and can be browsed and searched offline.

How do I change the language?

Crush Atlas supports Traditional Chinese and English. Use the app-language option in iPhone Settings; the exact menu depends on your iOS version.

Can the content prove someone likes me?

No. A single behavior cannot prove anyone's feelings. Consider the relationship, context, time, and multiple consistent signals, while respecting rejection, privacy, and consent.

Report a problem

Please email us with the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, steps that led to the problem, and any error message.

To protect everyone's privacy, do not send private conversations, photos of others without consent, addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or other sensitive personal information.
